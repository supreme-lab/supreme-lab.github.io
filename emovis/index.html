EMOVIS

A Visual Approach to Tracking Emotional Sentiment Dynamics in Social Network Commentaries

📄 Read Paper Explore System ↓
ICWSM 2024 · Proceedings

Overview

Social media comment sections are real-time barometers of public opinion. EMOVIS introduces a novel framework leveraging RoBERTalarge to predict emotional scores from user comments, mapping them to key sentiment trends — Approval, Toxicity, Obscenity, Threat, Hate, Offensive, and Neutral.

A bottom-up recursive algorithm aggregates emotional scores within comment threads. Our emotional model achieves an AUC of 0.92, and XGBoost leads with an F1 score exceeding 0.40.

Research Questions

Five Core Inquiries

RQ1

Temporal Sentiment Tracking

Can we design a tool to track the evolution of sentiment trends across different time frames — short-term fluctuations and long-term shifts?

RQ2

Real-Time Challenges

What challenges and opportunities arise when implementing real-time sentiment tracking and visualization in social media comment sections?

RQ3

BERT Accuracy

How effective is the BERT model at accurately predicting emotional scores from complex, ambiguous, and multifaceted social media comments?

RQ4

Direct vs. Two-Step

Which approach is more effective: direct trend prediction via T-BERT, or a two-step method using E-BERT + traditional ML models?

E-BERT (Emotion)

Fine-tuned RoBERTalarge on GoEmotion dataset (58k Reddit comments). Outputs 28 emotion probability scores per comment.

T-BERT (Trend)

Fine-tuned on combined Twitter + Wikipedia dataset (Dtrend). Predicts 8 trend categories: Approval, Toxic, Obscene, Threat, Insult, Hate, Offensive, Neutral.
